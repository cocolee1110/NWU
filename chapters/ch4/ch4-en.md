## Translation notes

P’ is an affectionate or respectful honorific for someone older. Muay is Patricia’s nickname for TingTing. “55555” and its variations are Thai internet laughter. Kub is a masculine polite particle used playfully in their English messages. The recurring Thai hup cue becomes “hold it in / keep it together.” “Don’t get all Font on me” preserves TingTing’s teasing comparison between Japp and Font. “Updating my Pat-ch” plays on Pat’s name and a software patch. “Dear Mommy” is teasing about caring for the younger woman, not biological parenthood. “Unknown Elders” follows the project’s group-name convention.

Existing English messages, the original Thai ship hashtag, emoji sequences and laughter are retained. The sole image-manifest entry is a call-interface icon, not a photograph. Its 00:01:05 duration has been recovered from the saved HTML and rendered as a call notice. This chapter requires no image files. No pictures were inspected or downloaded. The characters’ health, diet and alcohol statements are translated as fictional dialogue, not independent medical guidance. This complete corrected export replaces the earlier incomplete Chapter 4 translation.
