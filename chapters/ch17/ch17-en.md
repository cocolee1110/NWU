## Translation notes

P’ is an affectionate or respectful honorific for someone older. Muay is Patricia’s nickname for TingTing. “55555” and its variations are Thai internet laughter. “Sisters,” “older sister” and “younger sister” describe a close nonfamilial, age-based bond here, not biological siblings. The characters use “pure” to discuss their own understanding of affection; this is their perspective, not a judgment on romantic or LGBTQ+ love. “Hold on” continues the metaphor of not letting go of one another. The friends’ dramatic death jokes are retained. “Unknown Elders” follows the established coworker-group convention. Original English messages and the source’s invitation notification are retained as supplied.

The three manifest image files are call-interface icons, not conversation photographs. All seven call appearances have been replaced by readable notices, with durations recovered from saved HTML. There are no photograph references in this chapter. The generic location card is preserved as a pinned-location notice; no actual hospital address or coordinates were supplied. The TXT has “missed call” appended to two call markers; this agrees with the call content in the JSON and does not change the dialogue. No images were inspected or downloaded. Medical statements and advice remain fictional character dialogue, not independently verified guidance.
