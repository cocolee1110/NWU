## Translation notes

P’ is an affectionate or respectful honorific for someone older. Muay is Patricia’s nickname for TingTing. “55555” and its variations are Thai internet laughter. Their recurring hup self-composure cue becomes “hold it in / keep it together.” Ngao/ngon is rendered through loneliness or sulking as appropriate; coaxing someone means making it up to them after they sulk. “Husband” and “wife” retain the women’s playful relationship language without assigning a male gender to either woman. “Unknown Elders” follows the project’s established group-name rendering. C.I. refers to corporate identity in the work discussion. The relationship questions retain gender-neutral “partner” where the original does not specify a man or woman.

Existing English messages, emoji sequences, laughter, mentions and author’s notes are retained. Record order is unchanged, including the embedded Bell message repeated later in the other group. The sole image-manifest entry is a call-interface icon, not a photograph. Its 00:00:24 duration has been recovered from the saved HTML and rendered as a call notice. No image files are needed, and no pictures were inspected or downloaded. The characters’ diet and health statements are translated as fictional dialogue, not independent medical guidance. This translation uses the complete corrected export rather than the older truncated project copy.
